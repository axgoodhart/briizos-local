briizOS journals: four asks, five features
QOL push + the scroll toggle that invented itself · July 8, 2026

Aida brought four QOL requests for the journals component: trustworthy text pagination, a single-page view under 800px, adjustable line height, and unlockable heading fonts with access to her computer's font library. After research ruled prefab TipTap pagination a bad architectural fit, the bespoke engine was hardened instead: fixed page geometry, live bidirectional reflow with caret handoff, per-journal typography, and system fonts. Mid-session she proposed a per-folio infinite-scroll toggle, answered a clarifying question with a full spec of her own (folio/page vocabulary, Spatial vs Fluid folios), and the seamed-stack scroll view landed on top of the same engine. Everything parses and bundles; nothing has run yet.
